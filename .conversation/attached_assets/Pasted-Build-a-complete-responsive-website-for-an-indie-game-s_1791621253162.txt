## 1. Design Direction

Create a polished, minimalist indie game studio website with a light, warm, friendly, and slightly whimsical aesthetic.

Visual inspiration:

* Independent creative studios
* Modern editorial portfolio websites
* Minimalist Japanese-inspired design
* Soft, playful character design
* Premium indie game websites

The website should feel personal, creative, distinctive, and professional without looking corporate or childish.

### Color palette

* Warm ivory: #F7F3EB
* Cream white: #FFFDF8
* Dark charcoal brown: #40332F
* Soft peach: #F4B77A
* Warm beige: #E6D8C5
* Muted taupe: #A99A8B

Use warm ivory as the primary background, charcoal brown for text, and peach sparingly for highlights and interactive elements.

Avoid dark-mode-first layouts, neon colors, excessive gradients, glassmorphism, generic gaming aesthetics, and overly decorative UI.

### Typography

Use a clean, highly readable sans-serif typeface such as Manrope, DM Sans, or a similar modern font.

Use bold, expressive headings paired with simple body text. Typography should feel slightly playful but remain professional.

### Logo and mascot
